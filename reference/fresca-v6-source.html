(06) FAQ

Questions clients ask

What does a design project include?+

A full project includes a measured floor plan, a materials and finishes board, elevations for kitchens and baths, a lighting plan, and a product list with every item specified. Smaller projects can include only the pieces you need.

Do you manage the remodel, or only the design?+

We design and document the project, then work alongside your contractor through construction. If you don’t have a contractor yet, we can recommend ones we have worked with before.

How is a project priced?+

Design is billed as a flat fee based on the size and scope of the project, which we set after the first consultation. Construction and furnishings are paid directly to your contractor and vendors.

How long does a kitchen or bathroom take?+

Design usually takes four to eight weeks. Construction depends on the contractor and materials, but most kitchens take two to four months and most bathrooms six to ten weeks.

Do you take on single rooms?+

Yes. Many projects start with one kitchen or bathroom, and the process is the same whether it’s one room or the whole house.
